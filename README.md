# My Favorite TV Series

Here are some of my all-time favorite TV series. Each one has a unique story, strong characters, and great cinematography.

---

## 1. Sky Rojo
![Sky Rojo](https://upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/en/8/82/Sky_Rojo_poster.jpg)

**Genre:** Action, Crime, Drama  
**Created by:** Álex Pina & Esther Martínez Lobato  

> "Run, fight, survive."  
> Sky Rojo follows three women escaping from their pimp, embarking on a chaotic road trip full of danger and freedom.

**Why I like it:**
- Fast-paced and full of energy  
- Strong female leads  
- Bold visual style  

**More info:** [Sky Rojo on IMDb](https://www.imdb.com/title/tt10080732/)

---

## 2. American Horror Story
![American Horror Story](https://upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/en/3/3e/American_Horror_Story_logo.png)

**Genre:** Horror, Anthology  
**Created by:** Ryan Murphy & Brad Falchuk  

Each season tells a new and chilling story — from haunted houses to witch covens and freak shows.

> "Normal people scare me."

**Favorite Seasons:**
1. Asylum 
2. Freak Show
3. 1984

**More info:** [American Horror Story on FX](https://www.fxnetworks.com/shows/american-horror-story)

---

## 4. Euphoria
![Euphoria](https://upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/en/f/f2/Euphoria_Season_1_poster.png)

**Genre:** Drama, Coming-of-age  
**Created by:** Sam Levinson  

Euphoria explores the lives of high school students navigating love, identity, addiction, and trauma in a visually stunning way.

**Main Cast:**
- Zendaya as *Rue Bennett*  
- Hunter Schafer as *Jules Vaughn*  
- Sydney Sweeney as *Cassie Howard*

> "The most beautiful part of life is not knowing what's next."

**More info:** [Euphoria on HBO](https://www.hbo.com/euphoria)

---

### Final Thoughts
All four of these series stand out because of:
- Strong storytelling  
- Complex characters  
- Emotional depth  

Each one made me feel something different — fear, excitement, sadness, or hope. That’s why I love them.
